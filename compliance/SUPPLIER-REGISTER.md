## Supplier due diligence

Before a supplier receives customer information, record:

- legal identity, service scope, security contact and data protection contact;
- categories of information and purposes for processing;
- access, encryption, authentication and incident controls;
- locations of storage and remote support;
- subcontractors and their locations;
- deletion, export and return of information at termination;
- audit/evidence rights and breach notification terms;
- applicable transfer mechanism and required contract terms;
- service continuity, backup and exit arrangements;
- owner, risk decision and approval date.
